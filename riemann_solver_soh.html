Riemann solver for the SOH model

Get solutions of the Riemann problem associated to the Self-Organized Hydrodynamics (SOH) model, given by $$ \partial_t \rho + \partial_x(\rho\cos\theta) = 0,$$ $$\partial_t \theta + c\partial_x(\sin \theta) - \lambda \frac{\sin\theta}{\rho} \partial_x \rho = 0, $$ where $\rho$ is the density of individuals, $\theta$ the angle of their mean velocity, and $c$ and $\lambda$ are two constants depending on a noise coefficient $d$ (see [Motsch and Navoret] for the computation of $c$ and $\lambda$).

Fill in the noise coefficient and the left and right states, then compute the solution.

Initialisation in progress, a few more seconds…

Calculations done locally in the navigator via Pyodide. The first loading can take 20 to 45 seconds depending on the connexion.
